About the Club

KUET Theatres

KUET Theatres is a student dramatics club from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology. We work on acting, writing, stage performance, and teamwork. The club gives students a place to learn, perform, and enjoy theatre together.

Who We Are

We are a group of students who like theatre and stage work. Some of us act, some write, and some help behind the stage.

What We Do

We practice drama, prepare shows, take part in cultural programs, and try to keep theatre active on campus.

Our Goal

Our goal is to build confidence, creativity, and friendship through theatre and live performance.
